Laptops and Phones are such versatile devices that you could do anything with it. It's a blessing and a curse at the same time.

There is no concept of Work Mode, Study Mode, Entertainment Mode, with these devices.

So, you could do the next best thing. You could have a Work Laptop, Study Laptop, and Personal Laptop for everything else.

It's seems very wasteful, but this approach has done wonders for my productivity.

But, I like to take it one step futher.

Since I spend most of my time with a Web Browser, Code Editor, and Terminal, the main source of distraction comes from the Web Browser. Therefore, I have installed [Web Site blocker](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/web-site-blocker/aoabjfoanlljmgnohepbkimcekolejjn?hl=en) plugin on my Work and Study laptops. 

With Web Site blocker, I have blocked all the websites that I consider to be huge distractions. I've kept the list very short, just a handful of websites. 

This has worked wonders for my productivity and focus.
